# Vonwik TV Setup Guide

Setup notes for [Vonwik TV](https://vonwik.tv/) customers: how to install the player, log in, and fix the most common problems on each device.

Vonwik TV is an IPTV subscription service. One subscription covers live TV, sports, and movies and series on demand, on 1 to 5 devices at the same time.

## Before you start

You need three things, all sent by email after checkout:

1. Your **username**
2. Your **password**
3. The **server URL** (also called portal URL)

Keep that email. If you cannot find it, check the spam folder or sign in to your [billing account](https://vonwik.tv/billing/).

## Amazon Firestick and Android TV

1. On the Firestick home screen, open **Settings > My Fire TV > Developer options** and turn on **Install unknown apps** for the Downloader app.
2. Install **Downloader** from the Amazon Appstore.
3. Open Downloader and type `vonwik.tv/smarters` in the URL box, then press **Go**.
4. When the download ends, press **Install**, then **Open**.
5. Choose **Login with Xtream Codes API** and enter your username, password and server URL.

Full guide with screenshots: [Vonwik on Firestick](https://vonwik.tv/vonwik-firestick/).

## iPhone and iPad

1. Install an IPTV player from the App Store.
2. Add a new user with **Xtream Codes API**.
3. Enter your username, password and server URL.

Details: [Vonwik on iOS](https://vonwik.tv/vonwik-ios/).

## Smart TV and Roku

Samsung and LG TVs use a player app from the TV app store. Roku needs a different method, described here: [Vonwik on Roku](https://vonwik.tv/vonwik-roku/).

## Login checklist

| Field | What to enter |
|---|---|
| Any name | Anything you like, for example `Vonwik` |
| Username | Exactly as in the email, no spaces |
| Password | Exactly as in the email, capital letters matter |
| URL | The full server URL, starting with `http://` |

More help: [Vonwik login guide](https://vonwik.tv/vonwik-login/).

## Common problems

- **"Invalid login" or "Account expired"**: retype the details by hand (copy and paste often adds a space), then check your expiry date in the [billing account](https://vonwik.tv/billing/).
- **Buffering**: restart the router and the device, use 5 GHz Wi-Fi or a cable, and close other streams on the same line.
- **Channels missing or EPG empty**: in the player settings, refresh the channel list and the TV guide.
- **Too many devices**: each plan allows a fixed number of screens at the same time. Stop the stream on one device before starting another.

Step-by-step fixes: [Vonwik not working](https://vonwik.tv/vonwik-not-working/).

## Account and renewal

Sign in at [vonwik.tv/billing](https://vonwik.tv/billing/) with the email you used at checkout. You receive a one-time code by email, and from there you can see your plan, your expiry date, and renew the same plan.
